# VIP Finance — Uizard design direction

Create a completely new visual concept for VIP Finance while keeping the entire existing finance functionality unchanged.

KEEP: dashboard, operations, income/expense/transfer, accounts/cards, categories with custom icons/colors, budgets, analytics, converter, debts and interest, goals and top-ups, reminders, receipt OCR, local smart signals, settings, themes, backup/export and persistent storage.

VISUAL DIRECTION: expressive premium consumer fintech. Use a confident coral + electric violet + amber palette, rich layered cards, playful but professional rounded geometry, bold typography, colorful category/account identity and clear visual storytelling. The app should feel memorable and highly designed rather than like stock Material UI.

DESIGN THE WHOLE PRODUCT: Home, Operations, Accounts, Categories, Budgets, Analytics, Converter, Debts, Goals, Reminders, Receipts, Settings, onboarding-like empty states and all dialogs. Use strong card composition, visual progress, meaningful iconography, polished loading/empty states and distinct primary/secondary actions.

PERSONALIZATION: account and category icons/colors must be visually prominent and consistent. Charts and progress indicators should use the same visual language. Dark mode must be a true designed dark theme, not simply inverted colors.

ANDROID: Kotlin + Jetpack Compose + Material 3 foundations. Keep all business logic and data compatibility. The result must be production-ready, responsive and comfortable on a phone.
